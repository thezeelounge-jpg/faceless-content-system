# Task: plan the next 4 weeks of posts

Plan one post for each entry in `slots` (the input below). Our scheduler already chose these dates from the client's posting days and posts per week, so return exactly one post per entry, in the same order, and add no others. A date appears twice when that day has two posts; give those two posts different pillars.

For each post return:
- `date`: copied from `slots`.
- `pillar`: one of the names in `pillars`. Spread the pillars evenly across the month and never use the same pillar more than twice in a row.
- `title`: a short working title (under 10 words) for our team.
- `hook`: the opening line the twin will say, 12 words or fewer. It makes a promise or opens a question that this audience cares about.
- `format`: `talking` (face on screen, best for opinions, stories and advice) or `story` (b-roll and big captions between the hook and the call to action, best for lists, steps and how-tos). Use both formats across the month.

Make the month feel like a series, not 4 weeks of the same post: mix quick tips, myths, stories, opinions and questions from the audience. If `notes` are given (for example, Ramadan or a launch), follow them. During Ramadan, lean toward Ramadan-friendly topics for those dates and keep the tone respectful.
