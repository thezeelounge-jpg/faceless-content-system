# How to record your clip

This page is shown to customers before they record, and sent by email when a reshoot is needed. Your twin can only look as real as this clip. Ten minutes of care here saves you from every video looking "almost right".

## Before you start

- **Length:** about 2 minutes of you talking, in one continuous take. (The app tells you the exact minimum and maximum it accepts.)
- **Same look as your videos:** wear what you want to appear in. Solid colours work best. Avoid fine stripes, small checks and big logos.
- **Face fully visible:** keep hair off your face. No hat, sunglasses or tinted glasses.

## Camera

- Use your phone's **back camera**, at **1080p or 4K, 30 fps**.
- Hold the phone **upright (vertical)**, on a tripod or a stack of books, **at eye level**.
- Frame from **the top of your head to the middle of your chest**, centred, with a little space above your head.
- Turn **off** beauty mode, filters, portrait/blur mode and auto-zoom.

## Light

- **Face a window** in daylight, or put one soft light in front of you.
- **No bright window or lamp behind you.**
- Avoid coloured lights, and lights that flicker.

## Background

- Simple and tidy: a plain wall, a bookshelf or your office.
- **Nothing moving:** no fan, TV, screen, pets or people walking past.

## Sound

- A **quiet room** with soft things in it (curtains, a sofa, a rug) to stop echo.
- Turn off the AC and fans, and switch your phone to silent.
- A clip-on microphone is best. Otherwise keep the phone within an arm's length.

## How to talk

1. **Start by saying the 3 words shown in the app**, clearly, while looking at the camera. This is your consent check.
2. Then talk naturally about your work, your customers or a story, the way you would on a reel.
3. **Look into the lens,** not at the screen.
4. Keep hand movements small and **below your chin.** Never cover your mouth or face.
5. **Every few sentences, pause for one second with your lips closed.** This helps the twin look natural when it is silent.
6. Stay in place: no walking, turning away or leaning out of frame.
7. No eating, chewing gum or drinking during the take.

## Sending the clip

- Upload it **in the app**, or share it from **Google Drive.**
- **Do not send it through WhatsApp.** WhatsApp shrinks videos, and your twin will look blurry.

## Quick self-check before you upload

- [ ] I said the 3 words at the start
- [ ] My face is bright and sharp the whole time
- [ ] There is no echo or background noise
- [ ] Nobody else appears in the clip
- [ ] It is one continuous take of about 2 minutes

## What happens next

1. Our system checks the clip (length, sharpness, sound, one face). If something is wrong, you get a clear reason and tips.
2. Our team checks the consent words, then creates your twin.
3. You get an email from HeyGen asking you to confirm your consent on webcam. Please complete it within 24 hours.
4. We make short test videos of your twin and choose the most realistic look with you.
5. Your first videos arrive.
