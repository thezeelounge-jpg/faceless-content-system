# Competitor notes: Brandios (brandios.ai)

Read on 20 Nov 2026 from its pricing, AI-clone, content-autopilot and early-access pages. The homepage itself could not be fetched. These notes are for inspiration only. **Never copy their code, branding, wording or design.**

## Status

Still pre-launch ("opens in the coming weeks"). It is running a free waitlist and is already taking payment for 50 paid "founding" spots at 30% off. Made by NextVibe Tech L.L.C-FZ, Dubai. Your earlier research found it runs on Segmind (twin renders), Fish Audio (voice), ElevenLabs, OpenAI and Claude.

## What they offer

- **The twin:** one clip of up to 60 seconds, started with 3 random words that expire after 30 minutes (liveness check), after a consent tick.
- **Scenes:** 2 studios and 16 scenes (office, clinic, rooftop, pool and so on), plus split, corner and cutout layouts.
- **"Studio" videos:** the cloned voice narrating over pictures and stock clips, with no face. These run 30 to 90 s and are on the cheapest plan.
- **Autopilot:** 1 to 14 posts a week; built-in best hours per network and per audience country (up to 3 countries); UAE Friday and Ramadan rules; publishing to LinkedIn, Instagram, Facebook Pages, X and TikTok; Telegram for approvals and manual hand-off.
- **Approvals:** every twin video waits for a yes by default.
- **Weekly learning:** every Sunday it reviews what worked and adjusts topics, times and format mix.
- **Languages:** English variants, Arabic (Standard plus dialects) and about 12 European languages. No Urdu or Hindi.

- **Packs:** 10 twin videos (720p) for $39, or 10 studio videos for $19. Packs don't expire.
- **A 60-second video counts as 4.** AI pictures, remakes per picture and posts each have their own limits.
- **Trial and cancellation:** a 7-day trial with no card, up to 5 watermarked samples. A free plan keeps the twin and drafts. Cancel anytime, with a refund within 14 days on request.
- **Discounts:** waitlist 15% off for 3 months; founding 30% off for life (50 spots).

## What we're taking (already added to docs/PRODUCT.md)

- Built-in posting times per country, including Friday prayer time and Ramadan. Users never pick hours.
- A cost comparison on the landing page (what a videographer, editor and writer cost against our plan).
- The trust line "nothing with your face goes out without your OK", and "you own your videos".
- A clear refund and cancel answer in the FAQ.
- Voice-only reels as a likely next format for a cheaper plan.
- Pre-selling: their paid founding spots match our plan to sell done-for-you before the SaaS exists.

## What we're deliberately not doing

- **Counting in 15-second units, or making quality cost video count (720p vs Full HD).** Ours is "X videos, each up to Y seconds", always 1080p, always the most realistic engine.
- **Separate limits for pictures, remakes, studio videos and packs.** Too much to read.
- **Stacked offers** (waitlist + founding + trial). One offer at a time.
- **Fake studio scenes in v1.** Our twin is filmed in the creator's real space, which looks more real.
- **Leaving quality to the customer.** Our QA gates check every video first.

## For the pricing discussion

- **Their Full HD allowance is small.** Starter's 5 Full HD twin videos of 15 s is 75 seconds of video for $99. By your earlier document's price (about $4.83 a minute for HeyGen Avatar IV), 75 seconds would cost us about $6 to make. There is room to give far more realistic 1080p video at a similar or lower price.
- **Their entry price for a talking twin is $99** ($69.30 for founding members). Their cheapest plan ($49) has no face at all.
- **They don't cover Urdu, Hindi, Pakistani payments or done-for-you.** These are our openings.
